Title: fragments of "On the nature of Deamons" by Kalfu The Flamebearer, chapter 6 "What we fear", page 54

Deamon Realms, Entrapment, Manifestation of hell whatever name you wish to call this power it can, depending on the Deamons power, be the most dangerous or the weakest of their powers. This power allows them to change your weapons, your most priced possesions, the very ground you are standing on can be exchanged for something that can benefit your enemy. The weakest of demons can use it to make your gloves ten times or turn your weapon into a stick, most strong demons can use much more sofisticated version of this ability, they can create a space based on the place they are in.

There exists 2 types of what we call Deamon realms, a Labyrinth and a Battlefield. 
The Labyrinth is used by a Deamon to trap their enemies in order to buy them time usually to regenerate or escape. Labyrinth can never be impossible to escape, it always has to have a way to escape its grasp, usually the rules of reality are changed for example lighting all the candles can unlock a door or burning every book might make a wall disapear. no matter how much time passes a Labyrinth will never cease to exist unless the Deamon dispels it, whoever is trapped in it escapes or the Deamon dies.
