Title: fragments of "On the nature of Deamons" by Kalfu The Flamebearer, chapter 4 "The Deamonic anatomy", page 32

Deamons are able to regenerate from almost any wound, they are even able to regrow their  limbs or eyes. They can make this process faster by drinking human blood and eating human flesh that is why in local legends some Deamons are reffered to as vampires. They cannot die from old age or disease, the only way the can be killed is by destroying their  heart and head.
